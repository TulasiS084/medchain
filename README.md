🏥 MedChain — Secure Digital Prescription & Medical Record Platform

MedChain is a secure web-based platform where doctors issue digital prescriptions and patients maintain lifelong medical records in one place.
Only the doctor and the respective patient can view the prescription and records, ensuring privacy, security, and authenticity.

This system replaces handwritten prescriptions and scattered medical files with a centralized, tamper-proof, and easily accessible digital solution.

🚀 Features
👨‍⚕️ Doctor login & dashboard
🧑‍💼 Patient login & personal medical record space
📝 Doctors create and send digital prescriptions
🔒 Prescriptions visible only to the concerned doctor and patient
📁 Patients can store and view complete medical history
🕒 Timestamped records for every prescription
🔐 Secure authentication and data privacy
🌐 Web-based access from anywhere

🧠 Problem It Solves

Traditional prescriptions:

Can be lost or damaged
Are hard to track over time
Can be misread due to handwriting
Are not securely stored

MedChain provides:

Permanent digital records
Clear, readable prescriptions
Secure access control
Easy tracking of medical history
